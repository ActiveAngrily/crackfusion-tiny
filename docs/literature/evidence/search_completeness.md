It is useful for forward/backward citation chaining, not a classifier leaderboard: its tables aggregate image classification, detection, segmentation, and measurement. ## Boundary decisions

- **Direct core:** one image/crop yields crack/no-crack for concrete; SDNET2018; compact/local-global CNN–Transformer classification; split unit, domain transfer, or actual device deployment evidence.
- **Adjacent, not pooled as binary baselines:** joint surface-plus-crack labels (six-class SDNET), crack type/severity, asphalt-only data, segmentation/detection, measurement, and structural diagnosis. These can inform controls, deployment, or scope wording but their metrics are not comparable to binary SDNET classification.
- **Hybrid taxonomy:** convolutional attention modules alone (e.g. MobileNetV3-C//Sim) are compact attention CNNs, not CNN–Transformer hybrids. Serial CNN→Transformer (e.g. CCTNet, MobileNetV3→LeViT), parallel CNN/attention fusion, and Transformer backbones with convolutional modules should be named distinctly.
- **Generalization:** random/stratified tile splits are not evidence of source-photo independence. “No image content leakage” needs a defined duplicate/parent grouping procedure. Cross-dataset scores need overlap and label-lineage checks, especially where composite/conglomerate sets can contain known public sources.
- **Deployment:** parameters/FLOPs, desktop FPS, edge profiling, and end-to-end system latency are different measurements. Do not rank without hardware, batch, precision, input, runtime, and timing-boundary details.
